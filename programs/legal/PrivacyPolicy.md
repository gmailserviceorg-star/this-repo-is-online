# Stratum OS Privacy Policy
1. Nothing leaves your device unless a data-collection toggle is ON (see setup step 7) or you start a download or update.
2. Each toggle lists exactly what it sends, with a sample payload.
3. "Disable data collecting" turns every toggle off; after that nothing is sent.
4. Crash reports stay local unless you consent per report.
5. Logs (audit, crash) live under /var/log and can be deleted by you.
6. Scroll to the end of this page to accept.
